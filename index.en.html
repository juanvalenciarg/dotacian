In-house manufacturing

We make your uniforms.

We're not just software. We design and manufacture your company's uniforms, with your logo and in your people's real sizes — and manage them with you in the same platform.

  • Garments for every role — chef coats, coveralls, scrubs, polos, shirts, trousers, aprons, footwear and PPE.
  • Your brand on every piece — embroidered or printed logo, colors and fabrics fitted to your operation.
  • Visible production — follow every order from the sewing floor to dispatch in the platform.
  • Quality and the right sizes — we produce from your roster's sizes, not from guesses.
